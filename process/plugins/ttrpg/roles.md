## One Stance

### Game Master

All-in-one: world design, session prep, NPC management, rules arbitration. Design and execution are fluid within a single conversation — no handoffs, no separate design stance. This validates that core's multi-stance infrastructure doesn't force unnecessary ceremony on simple domains.

**When you use it:** All work. The Game Master is the only stance.
